Section 1: Executive Strategy & Core Ideology
1.1 Vision Statement
FicForge is a home for fanfiction writers and original authors alike—a platform where stories come first, communities form naturally around those stories, and creators have the tools they need to share their worlds with readers. Dedicated to transformative fiction, fandom storytelling, and serialized web fiction, FicForge empowers writers to publish, grow their audiences, and build lasting connections through the stories they love to tell.
1.2 Mission Statement
To provide writers and readers with a dedicated storytelling platform that prioritizes creativity, creator ownership, community engagement, and sustainable growth while fostering an environment where stories can thrive.
1.3 Core Operating Philosophies
Stories First: The reading and writing experience is never compromised. No intrusive pop-ups, mid-chapter distractions, or broken text lines.
Creators Matter: Authors retain full copyright ownership of original intellectual property and receive highly competitive compensation splits.
Organic Communities: Discussions and social spaces are built directly around the text to drive deep, collaborative engagement.
Accessibility: Zero barriers to entry for reading or writing. Anyone with an internet connection can consume or publish material.
Long-Term Sustainability: A business model engineered to be highly profitable and durable without compromising community ethics.

2.1 The Fanfiction Framework
Monetization Ban: Direct sales or paywalling of individual fanfiction chapters is completely blocked by platform core logic.
Legally Defensible Income: Fanfiction monetization is strictly limited to General Creator Appreciation. Readers can tip an author’s public profile using the site-wide token economy ("Sparks") or utilize outbound platform widgets linking to external, third-party profile accounts (Patreon/Ko-fi).
Platform Defense: The website acts strictly as a neutral, free digital repository. If a corporate copyright holder issues an official takedown request, the platform deletes the reported file instantly, fully preserving its legal immunity.

2.2 The Original Fiction Framework
Monetization Unlocked: Original webnovels are granted unrestricted e-commerce access.
The Fast Pass System: Authors can lock advanced chapters behind a token wall, which automatically opens on a timed countdown schedule for free readers.
Secondary Paywalls: Authors can establish direct, recurring monthly cash subscription tiers for exclusive portfolio access.

Section 3: Technical Architecture (Three-Tier Framework)
FicForge is structured around a standard Three-Tier Architecture, engineered completely without custom coding by combining WordPress with specialized logic plugins.
3.1 Presentation Layer (The Frontend Interface)
Infrastructure Components: Astra WordPress Theme + Elementor Visual Page Builder.
Function: Delivers a highly responsive, clean reading interface optimized for mobile and desktop screens. Controls dark mode toggles, margins, padding, and text typography formatting.
3.2 Logic Layer (The Backend Application)
Infrastructure Components: WordPress Core + Ultimate Member + Paid Memberships Pro.
Function: Dictates user permissions and structural rules. Confirms user login states, calculates token balances, manages chapter-gating execution, and handles account security.
3.3 Data Layer (The Database Warehouse)
Infrastructure Components: MySQL Database (Automated via standard web hosting).
Function: Operates as the secure digital filing cabinet. Links individual chapter texts to their parent book folders, separates metadata tags, and houses user account records securely.



Section 4: Interface Configuration & Workflow Layout
4.1 Publishing Workflow Data Fields
When a writer initiates the "New Story" command, a frontend submission form requires the completion of specific database fields:
Core Details: Title, Story Synopsis (500-word limit), and Classification Selection (Fanfiction vs. Original).
Fanfiction Conditional Routing: Selecting "Fanfiction" disables all chapter-locking logic and triggers the following metadata inputs: Fandom Source, Crossover Selection, and Relationship Classifiers (M/M, F/F, F/M, Gen).
Original Fiction Conditional Routing: Selecting "Original Fiction" unlocks monetization dropdowns: Genre Checkboxes (Fantasy, LitRPG, Sci-Fi, Romance, etc.) and Monetization Tier (Free, Fast Pass, Subscription Exclusive).
Global Metadata: Content Rating Selection (G, T, M, E), Content Warnings, Story Status Toggle (Ongoing vs. Completed), and Indexing Search Tags.
The Chapter Editor: A clean rich-text canvas optimized for copy-pasting text from Google Docs or Microsoft Word without losing paragraph indentation. Includes separate text boxes for Top and Bottom Author Notes.
4.2 Community & Ad Integration Guidelines
The Chapter Sanctuary Rule: Advertisements are strictly forbidden inside the body of a story chapter. Ad network integrations are explicitly restricted to the platform homepage, directory search feeds, sidebars, and the absolute bottom of pages beneath the user comments.
Text-Centric Commenting: Powered by the wpDiscuz thread plugin. Standard comment fields are transformed into interactive reader forums directly at the foot of each chapter to drive instant community interaction.

Section 6: Content Moderation & Safety Compliance
6.1 Zero-Tolerance Bans
The following violations result in immediate account deletion and text erasure:
Plagiarism, scraping, or unapproved compilation of other authors' works.
Real-Person Fiction (RPF) that inflicts active real-world harm or targeted defamation.
Any text depicting or normalizing illegal exploitation, hate speech, or real-world harm.

6.2 The Age Rating Threshold & Explicit Content Rule
Stories carrying an Explicit (E) rating are locked behind an initial 18+ age verification popup screen.
To maintain absolute compliance with mainstream payment networks (Stripe/PayPal), Explicit content is strictly banned from using the Sparks transaction engine, Fast Pass tools, or internal profile-tipping triggers. Explicit content must remain 100% free to access.

The Anatomical Boundary: Mature (M) vs. Explicit (E)
Mature (M): Heavy profanity, complex real-world themes (addiction, trauma), and standard action violence. Sexual content is permitted only if it utilizes "fade to black" transitions or focuses entirely on emotional/sensual descriptions rather than graphic physical anatomy.
Explicit (E): Intended strictly for adult audiences. This rating applies the moment a text contains highly graphic, detailed, or prolonged anatomical descriptions of sexual acts, or extreme visceral descriptions of gore and physical torture.

Section 8: Community Moderator Handbook
8.1 The Core Mission of FicForge Moderation
As a FicForge Community Moderator, your role is not to censor creative expression, but to protect the structural integrity and legal safety of the platform. Your primary objectives are to enforce accurate Content Rating Tags, ensure compliance with global Payment Processor Rules, and maintain a respectful community environment in the chapter comments section.
8.2 The Moderator Code of Conduct
Objective Neutrality: Review content based strictly on our written policies, never on your personal likes, dislikes, or opinions of a specific fandom or genre.
Confidentiality: All internal moderation discussions, user reports, and creator communications must remain strictly confidential. Never share mod-log data publicly.
Transparency and Appeals: When taking action against a story or account, always provide the creator with a clear, specific written reason from this handbook. Every creator has the right to a fair appeal.
The De-escalation Rule: Do not engage in arguments with users in the comment sections. If a situation becomes hostile, issue the appropriate platform warning or ban and close the ticket.
8.3 Report Review Protocol
When a reader clicks the "Report Content" flag on a story or comment, the ticket lands in your Moderation Dashboard. Follow these exact verification steps for every single ticket:
Check for Fatal Violations: Immediately review the text for zero-tolerance offenses. If the story contains verified plagiarism, AI-scraped text theft, or malicious real-person defamation, permanently delete the story, ban the account, and log the evidence.
Perform the "Anatomical Detail" Assessment: If a story is reported for a "Mislabeled Rating," look closely at how the scenes are written to check the boundary between Mature (M) and Explicit (E) using the guidelines in Section 6.2.
Check Monetization Compliance: If you find that a story should be rated Explicit (E) but is currently tagged as Mature (M), check its monetization status. If it is free, manually edit the tag to Explicit (E). If it is currently using the Fast Pass system or has active Spark Tipping turned on, move to the enforcement action.
Execute Correction Action: Freeze monetization by manually switching the story status to "Draft Mode." Send the automated system notice to the author to allow them to correct the tag or edit down the graphic anatomical text to meet Mature standards.

How do I earn money on FicForge?
Creators can monetize their presence on the platform through our site-wide virtual token ecosystem (Sparks) and custom cash subscriptions:

Original Webnovel Authors: You can lock advanced chapters behind a token-based Fast Pass system or set up a secondary, recurring monthly cash subscription tier for your portfolio.
What is the revenue split?
FicForge features a highly competitive 82/18 revenue split. For every transaction processed through your stories or profile—whether via Sparks or direct subscriptions—82% goes directly to you, and 18% goes to FicForge to maintain servers, cover operational software, and fund platform growth.
How are Sparks converted into real money?
Every Spark you receive carries a fixed cash value based on our global platform index:
100 Sparks in your balance translates to $1.00 in your earnings vault.
When a reader purchases token bundles, credit card transaction fees are baked into the consumer cost upfront. This ensures that when they tip you, your 82% share remains pure, un-eroded profit.
What are the requirements to cash out?
To initiate a bank transfer from your earnings vault, your account must meet two simple administrative requirements:
Minimum Payout Threshold: Your balance must hold a minimum value of $50.00 (equivalent to 5,000 Sparks).
Verified Creator Profile: You must complete a secure digital tax and banking onboarding profile through our standard payment processing partner (Stripe).
When do I get paid?
To keep platform banking overhead low and predictable, payouts are not automated instantly. Instead, withdrawals are securely batched and processed twice a month on a fixed calendar schedule:
Payout requests submitted between the 1st and 14th of the month are processed on the 15th.
Payout requests submitted between the 15th and the end of the month are processed on the 1st of the following month.
Once processed by the platform, funds generally take 2 to 5 business days to appear in your linked checking account, depending on your local banking institution.

1. The Fast Pass Timer System (How It Works)
The Fast Pass system lets original webnovel authors make money from impatient readers while keeping the story free for patient ones. Using a Points and Content Restriction plugin in WordPress, the system is fully automated.
The Author’s Dashboard Setup
When posting a new chapter of an original webnovel, the author fills out three fields:
Access Level: [x] Fast Pass
Fast Pass Cost: [ 50 ] Sparks
Release Timer: [ 7 ] Days
The Automation Cycle
The Lock Phase: The moment the author hits "Publish," the chapter is hidden behind a paywall window. A reader who clicks it sees: "This chapter is locked behind a Fast Pass. Spend 50 Sparks to read it now, or wait 7 days for it to unlock for free."
The Transaction: If the reader clicks "Unlock," 50 Sparks are deducted from their wallet. The system logs the 82/18 split (the author gets credit for 41 Sparks, FicForge keeps 9 Sparks).
The Auto-Unlock: Exactly 168 hours (7 days) after publication, the WordPress database changes the chapter's access level from "Fast Pass" to "Public." It is now 100% free for everyone, and the next chapter in line becomes the active Fast Pass chapter.

That adjustment optimizes the platform flow perfectly. By making Sparks the primary currency for both fanfiction and original webnovels, you create a unified ecosystem.
Readers buy one token balance and use it everywhere. On original fiction, instead of a cash paywall, a premium chapter simply costs a set number of Sparks (e.g., "50 Sparks to unlock Chapter 10 early"). Direct cash paywalls become an optional secondary feature for authors who prefer traditional monthly subscriptions.
