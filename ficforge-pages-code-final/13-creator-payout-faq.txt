  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Basics</span>
    <h2>How creators earn on FicForge</h2>
    <p>
      Creators may earn through eligible creator-support features, Sparks-based support, and future original-webnovel monetization tools. Earnings depend on the type of content, platform rules, account eligibility, and payout availability.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-two">
      <div class="ff-payout-box">
        <strong>Original Webnovels</strong>
        <p>
          Original webnovel creators may become eligible for broader monetization features as FicForge grows, including creator support, future premium tools, and approved story-based monetization.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="ff-payout-box">
        <strong>Fanfiction Creators</strong>
        <p>
          Fanfiction chapters cannot be sold, locked, Fast Passed, or offered as paid early access. Support connected to fanfiction creators must remain general profile support, not payment for a specific fanfic.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Spark Conversion</span>
    <h2>How Sparks convert into creator earnings</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge uses a creator-side Spark conversion rate to calculate eligible creator earnings.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-highlight">
      <p>
        <strong>Creator conversion:</strong> 100 Sparks = $1.00<br>
        <strong>Payout threshold:</strong> 5,000 Sparks = $50.00<br>
        <strong>Minimum payout:</strong> Creators must reach the payout threshold before requesting withdrawal.
      </p>
    </div>

    <p style="margin-top:14px;">
      The consumer price of Sparks may differ from the creator conversion rate because purchase pricing may account for platform operations, payment processing fees, fraud prevention, refunds, taxes, and service costs.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Payout Threshold</span>
    <h2>When creators can request a payout</h2>
    <p>
      A creator must reach the minimum payout threshold before a payout request can be processed.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Minimum threshold</strong>
        <p>Creators must have at least 5,000 eligible Sparks, equal to $50.00 in creator earnings, before requesting a payout.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Eligible balance</strong>
        <p>Only cleared and eligible earnings count toward payout. Pending, disputed, refunded, reversed, or held earnings may not be available for withdrawal.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Unreached threshold</strong>
        <p>If a creator has not reached the threshold, the balance remains in the creator account until enough eligible earnings are available.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Payout Schedule</span>
    <h2>When payouts are processed</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge plans to process eligible creator payouts semi-monthly.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Processing days</strong>
        <p>Eligible payouts are planned for the 1st and 15th of each month.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Review time</strong>
        <p>Payouts may require review before processing, especially for new creators, unusual activity, copyright reports, policy concerns, refund issues, or account verification problems.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Bank/payment delays</strong>
        <p>After FicForge processes a payout, the final arrival time may depend on the payout provider, bank, region, holidays, and account status.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Payment Methods</span>
    <h2>How creators may receive payouts</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge may support multiple payout methods as the platform develops. Availability may depend on the creator’s country, account verification, payment processor rules, and platform rollout stage.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Possible payout methods</strong>
        <p>FicForge may support Stripe Connect, PayPal Payouts, bank transfer, direct transfer, or other approved payment methods in the future.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Verification may be required</strong>
        <p>Creators may need to verify identity, payment account ownership, tax information, region eligibility, or other required details before receiving payouts.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Availability may vary</strong>
        <p>Not every payout method will be available to every creator immediately. FicForge may roll out payout options in stages.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Fanfiction Limits</span>
    <h2>Fanfiction creator support rules</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge allows fanfiction as community storytelling, but fanfiction monetization must be handled carefully.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Fanfiction chapters remain free</strong>
        <p>Fanfiction chapters cannot be sold, locked behind payment, placed behind Fast Pass, offered as paid early access, or used as paid rewards.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Profile support must stay general</strong>
        <p>Readers may support a creator’s overall public profile as general appreciation for that creator’s presence on FicForge, not for any specific fanfiction story, chapter, fandom, update, continuation, ship, character, bonus scene, or reward.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>No paid fanfiction promises</strong>
        <p>Creators may not promise fanfiction updates, bonus chapters, continuations, private chapters, commissions, or special fandom rewards in exchange for Sparks, subscriptions, tips, donations, or outside payments.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Original Fiction</span>
    <h2>Original webnovel monetization</h2>
    <p>
      Original webnovels are treated differently from fanfiction because they are based on the creator’s own characters, setting, world, and core concept.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Eligible original works</strong>
        <p>Original webnovel creators may become eligible for story-based monetization features when those tools are available and their account meets platform requirements.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Platform share</strong>
        <p>FicForge may keep a platform share from eligible original-fiction monetization to support hosting, tools, payment processing, moderation, and platform operations.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Creator responsibility</strong>
        <p>Creators are responsible for making sure their original work does not copy, plagiarize, or infringe another person’s protected work.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-payout-panel">
    <span class="label">Holds & Reviews</span>
    <h2>Why earnings or payouts may be held</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge may delay, hold, reduce, reverse, or deny payouts when required for safety, payment review, copyright enforcement, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-payout-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Payment disputes or refunds</strong>
        <p>Earnings connected to refunded, disputed, charged back, reversed, or suspicious transactions may be held or removed from the eligible payout balance.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Copyright or plagiarism reports</strong>
        <p>Accounts under serious copyright, plagiarism, reposting, or impersonation review may have earnings held while FicForge reviews the issue.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Policy violations</strong>
        <p>Content Policy violations, ban evasion, fake engagement, payment abuse, or misleading monetization may affect payout eligibility.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Verification problems</strong>
        <p>Payouts may be delayed if creator identity, payout method, tax information, account ownership, or regional eligibility cannot be verified.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
