  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Basics</span>
    <h2>What are Sparks?</h2>
    <p>
      Sparks are a FicForge platform currency that readers may use through approved FicForge features. They make it easier to support creators without making a separate payment every time.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-two">
      <div class="ff-sparks-box">
        <strong>For readers</strong>
        <p>
          Sparks let readers support creators, participate in future platform features, and manage support from one FicForge wallet.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="ff-sparks-box">
        <strong>For creators</strong>
        <p>
          Sparks may contribute to eligible creator earnings when used through approved support features and when the creator account meets payout requirements.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Spark Packs</span>
    <h2>How much do Sparks cost?</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge uses simple Spark packs so readers can choose how much they want to add to their account.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-highlight">
      <p>
        <strong>Starter:</strong> 100 Sparks — $1<br>
        <strong>Standard:</strong> 1,000 Sparks — $10<br>
        <strong>Supporter:</strong> 5,000 Sparks + 500 bonus — $50<br>
        <strong>Power Reader:</strong> 8,000 Sparks + 1,200 bonus — $80<br>
        <strong>Patron:</strong> 15,000 Sparks + 2,500 bonus — $125
      </p>
    </div>

    <p style="margin-top:14px;">
      Bonus Sparks are promotional extras included with larger packs. Spark prices may account for platform operations, payment processing, fraud prevention, refunds, taxes, and service costs.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Using Sparks</span>
    <h2>What can Sparks be used for?</h2>
    <p>
      Sparks may be used only through approved FicForge features. The exact features may expand over time as the platform develops.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-list">
      <div>
        <strong>General creator support</strong>
        <p>Readers may use Sparks to support a creator’s public profile as general appreciation for their presence and writing activity on FicForge.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Original webnovel features</strong>
        <p>Original webnovels may become eligible for approved monetization features as FicForge expands its creator tools.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Future platform features</strong>
        <p>Sparks may later support approved features such as event participation, reader boosts, creator tools, profile upgrades, or other FicForge-native features.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Limits</span>
    <h2>What Sparks cannot buy</h2>
    <p>
      Sparks are not a way to bypass FicForge’s content, copyright, fanfiction, or payment rules.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-list">
      <div>
        <strong>No paid fanfiction chapters</strong>
        <p>Sparks cannot be used to buy, unlock, Fast Pass, request, commission, or receive paid early access to fanfiction chapters.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>No specific fanfiction rewards</strong>
        <p>Sparks cannot be exchanged for a specific fanfiction story, chapter, fandom, update, continuation, ship, character, bonus scene, private scene, or reward.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>No rule-breaking content</strong>
        <p>Sparks cannot be used to purchase, encourage, or reward content that violates FicForge’s Content Policy, copyright rules, payment rules, or safety rules.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Fanfiction Boundary</span>
    <h2>How Sparks work around fanfiction</h2>
    <p>
      Fanfiction chapters on FicForge remain free to read. They cannot be sold, locked behind payment, placed behind Fast Pass, offered as paid early access, or used as paid rewards.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Allowed</strong>
        <p>General profile support for a creator’s overall public presence on FicForge, as long as it is not tied to any specific fanfiction work or reward.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Not allowed</strong>
        <p>Promising fanfiction chapters, continuations, bonus scenes, fandom requests, private updates, commissions, or special rewards in exchange for Sparks or payment.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Why this rule exists</strong>
        <p>This boundary helps FicForge separate general creator support from selling or monetizing third-party fanfiction content.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Creator Conversion</span>
    <h2>Do creators receive the same amount readers pay?</h2>
    <p>
      No. Reader purchase pricing and creator-side earnings are related, but they are not the same thing.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-highlight">
      <p>
        <strong>Reader purchase example:</strong> 1,000 Sparks costs $10<br>
        <strong>Creator-side conversion:</strong> 100 eligible Sparks = $1.00<br>
        <strong>Creator payout threshold:</strong> 5,000 eligible Sparks = $50.00
      </p>
    </div>

    <p style="margin-top:14px;">
      For payout rules, payout timing, holds, account review, payment methods, and creator tax responsibility, visit the Creator Payout FAQ.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Refunds & Reversals</span>
    <h2>Can Sparks be refunded?</h2>
    <p>
      Refunds may depend on payment status, payment processor rules, platform review, account activity, fraud concerns, and whether Sparks have already been used.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Unused Sparks</strong>
        <p>Refund requests for unused Sparks may be reviewed by FicForge according to platform policy and payment processor rules.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Used Sparks</strong>
        <p>Sparks that have already been used for support or platform features may not always be refundable.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Chargebacks and disputes</strong>
        <p>Payment disputes, chargebacks, refunds, or reversals may affect account balances, creator earnings, and payout eligibility.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-sparks-panel">
    <span class="label">Safety</span>
    <h2>Abuse prevention and account review</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge may review Spark activity to protect readers, creators, and the platform from fraud, abuse, manipulation, copyright issues, and payment misuse.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-sparks-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Suspicious activity</strong>
        <p>Unusual purchases, fake support patterns, refund abuse, chargebacks, stolen payment methods, or coordinated abuse may trigger review.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Creator account review</strong>
        <p>Creator earnings connected to suspicious Spark activity may be delayed, held, reversed, or removed from eligible payout balance.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Platform action</strong>
        <p>FicForge may restrict wallets, pause payouts, remove access to monetized tools, or suspend accounts when Spark abuse is found.</p>
