    <p>

    </p>

    <strong>Important Notice</strong>
    <p>
      This page explains FicForge’s copyright reporting process. It is not legal advice. Copyright notices and counter-notices can have legal consequences, so users should only submit truthful, accurate, good-faith claims.
    </p>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Our Position</span>
    <h2>FicForge respects copyright</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge is a user-generated storytelling platform. Creators are responsible for the content they upload, publish, or share through their accounts. FicForge does not allow users to upload stolen works, repost another creator’s writing without permission, impersonate copyright owners, or claim ownership of content they did not create.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-dmca-two">
      <div class="ff-dmca-box">
        <strong>Original Works</strong>
        <p>
          Creators keep ownership of their original stories, characters, settings, and creative writing, subject to the limited permissions needed for FicForge to host, display, distribute, and operate the platform.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="ff-dmca-box">
        <strong>Fanfiction Works</strong>
        <p>
          Fanfiction may involve third-party worlds, characters, fandoms, or source material. FicForge does not claim ownership of those third-party properties, and creators are responsible for respecting applicable copyright rules.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Takedown Notices</span>
    <h2>How to submit a copyright notice</h2>
    <p>
      To request removal of content you believe infringes your copyright, send a written notice to FicForge’s designated copyright agent. The notice should be clear, complete, and specific enough for FicForge to identify the content and review the claim.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-dmca-list">
      <div>
        <strong>1. Your contact information</strong>
        <p>Include your full legal name, organization if applicable, mailing address, phone number, and email address.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>2. The copyrighted work</strong>
        <p>Identify the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed. For multiple works, provide a representative list or clear description.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>3. The allegedly infringing content</strong>
        <p>Provide the FicForge URL, story title, chapter title, username, screenshots if helpful, and any other information that allows FicForge to locate the material.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>4. Good-faith statement</strong>
        <p>State that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>5. Accuracy and authority statement</strong>
        <p>State that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>6. Signature</strong>
        <p>Include a physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Review Process</span>
    <h2>What happens after FicForge receives a notice</h2>
    <p>
      After receiving a copyright notice, FicForge may review the report, remove or disable access to the reported material, notify the affected creator, request additional information, or take further action when needed.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-dmca-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Content removal or access restriction</strong>
        <p>Reported material may be removed or disabled while the copyright issue is reviewed.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Creator notification</strong>
        <p>FicForge may notify the creator whose content was removed or disabled and provide information about the claim where appropriate.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Account review</strong>
        <p>FicForge may review the creator’s account history, prior notices, reposting behavior, monetization activity, and related reports.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Further action</strong>
        <p>Depending on the situation, FicForge may restrict publishing, suspend monetization, remove content, suspend accounts, or terminate repeat infringer accounts.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Counter-Notices</span>
    <h2>How to submit a counter-notice</h2>
    <p>
      If your content was removed or disabled because of a copyright notice and you believe the removal was caused by mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice to FicForge’s designated copyright agent.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-dmca-list">
      <div>
        <strong>1. Identify the removed material</strong>
        <p>Provide the story title, chapter title, URL if available, username, and a description of the material that was removed or disabled.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>2. Provide your contact information</strong>
        <p>Include your full legal name, mailing address, phone number, and email address.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>3. Good-faith mistake statement</strong>
        <p>State that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled because of mistake or misidentification.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>4. Consent to jurisdiction</strong>
        <p>Include the required consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate federal district court, or another applicable jurisdiction statement if required by law.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>5. Acceptance of service</strong>
        <p>State that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original notice or their authorized agent.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>6. Signature</strong>
        <p>Include a physical or electronic signature.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Restoration</span>
    <h2>What happens after a counter-notice</h2>
    <p>
      After receiving a valid counter-notice, FicForge may forward it to the original complaining party. If the complaining party does not notify FicForge that they have filed a court action seeking to restrain the disputed activity, FicForge may restore the removed or disabled material after the legally required waiting period.
    </p>

    <p style="margin-top:12px;">
      Submitting a counter-notice is a serious legal step. Do not submit one casually, dishonestly, or without understanding the possible consequences.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Plagiarism & Reposting</span>
    <h2>Reports that are not formal DMCA notices</h2>
    <p>
      Some reports may involve plagiarism, reposted writing, copied summaries, stolen cover art, impersonation, or creator disputes that do not arrive as formal DMCA notices. FicForge may still review these reports under platform rules.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-dmca-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Examples of reportable behavior</strong>
        <p>Reposting another creator’s chapters, copying an original story without permission, uploading stolen cover art, impersonating an author, or falsely claiming ownership of another person’s work.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Helpful evidence</strong>
        <p>Provide links to the original work, links to the copied work, screenshots, upload dates, usernames, and a clear explanation of what was copied.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Platform action</strong>
        <p>FicForge may remove content, warn users, restrict features, suspend publishing, or take account action depending on the severity and evidence.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Repeat Infringers</span>
    <h2>Repeat copyright violations</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge may take stronger action against users who repeatedly upload infringing material, repost other creators’ work, ignore copyright warnings, evade removals, or create new accounts to continue violating copyright rules.
    </p>

    <div class="ff-dmca-list">
      <div>
        <strong>Possible consequences</strong>
        <p>Content removal, publishing restrictions, account warnings, monetization suspension, payout review, account suspension, or permanent account termination.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Monetization impact</strong>
        <p>Creators involved in copyright abuse, reposting, or plagiarism may lose access to Sparks, payouts, premium creator tools, or other monetized features.</p>
      </div>

      <div>
        <strong>Ban evasion</strong>
        <p>Creating a new account to avoid copyright enforcement may result in additional removals, account restrictions, or termination.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">False Claims</span>
    <h2>Do not submit false or abusive notices</h2>
    <p>
      Copyright notices and counter-notices must be truthful. Submitting false, misleading, abusive, or bad-faith copyright claims may have legal consequences and may also result in FicForge account action.
    </p>

    <p style="margin-top:12px;">
      Do not use the DMCA process to harass creators, silence criticism, remove content you simply dislike, attack competitors, or make claims over works you do not own or represent.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="ff-dmca-panel">
    <span class="label">Fanfiction Boundary</span>
    <h2>Fanfiction and third-party rights</h2>
    <p>
      FicForge allows fanfiction as transformative community storytelling, but fanfiction may involve third-party intellectual property. FicForge does not own those third-party worlds, characters, settings, or source materials.
    </p>

    <p style="margin-top:12px;">
      Fanfiction creators remain responsible for following copyright law, FicForge rules, and any applicable rights-holder restrictions. FicForge may remove or restrict fanfiction content if required by a valid copyright notice, platform policy, payment processor concern, or legal obligation.
    </p>
